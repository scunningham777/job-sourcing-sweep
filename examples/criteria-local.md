# Job search criteria – local or contract, quick start

Screening reads this whole file and judges every lead against it.

## Hard filters (exclude if failed)

1. **Location.** Fully remote in the US, or hybrid/on-site within about 45 minutes of
   Springfield, IL.
2. **Pay.** W-2 contract at roughly $55/hr or more, or full-time base around $110K or more.
   Keep postings with no rate listed, and say to confirm the rate.
3. **Can start within a few weeks.** Exclude roles with long, multi-stage hiring processes
   described in the posting.
4. **Front-end or full-stack.** Exclude roles that are mainly backend, QA, or support.

## Positive signals (not required, but count in favor)

- W-2 rather than 1099 or corp-to-corp
- Contract-to-hire, or a contract of 6 months or more
- TypeScript, React, or Angular named in the core stack

## Notes

- Note employment type (W-2, 1099, C2C, C2H, full-time) and contract length for every lead.
